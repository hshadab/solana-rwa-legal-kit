# Contributing

This project publishes legal materials in the open so that they can be corrected by people who know more than the author about any given piece of it. Corrections are more valuable here than new content.

## Most useful contributions

1. **Mechanism corrections.** If an entry in the [transfer restriction schedule](docs/transfer-restriction-schedule.md) assigns a restriction to the wrong enforcement mechanism, or if a program parameter is wrong or has changed, say so. This is the highest-value correction in the repository.
2. **Filling verification gaps.** Cells marked *TBD* need configuration detail verified against the Metaplex documentation or a live deployment. Please cite the source.
3. **Practice notes from real offerings.** If you have taken a permissioned asset to mainnet on Solana and something here would not have worked, that is worth an issue even without a proposed fix.
4. **EU coverage.** The MiCA, DLT Pilot, and Prospectus Regulation entries would benefit from review by counsel qualified in a member state.
5. **Jurisdictions outside scope.** Proposals to add a jurisdiction are welcome, but only with a contributor willing to maintain it.

## Ground rules

- **No confidential or client-identifying information.** Do not post anything from a matter you worked on that is not public. Generalize.
- **Cite authority.** A change to a legal statement should cite the rule, release, or guidance it rests on. A change to a mechanism should cite documentation or a verifiable on-chain example.
- **Date everything.** Legal content carries an effective date. If you change substance, update the date on that file.
- **Keep advice out.** This repository publishes templates and mappings, not opinions on anyone's offering. Issues asking whether a specific deal is compliant will be closed with a pointer to this file.

## No attorney-client relationship

Contributing to or using this repository does not create an attorney-client relationship with the author or any contributor, and nothing here is legal advice. Contributors are not providing legal services to users of these materials.

## Licensing of contributions

By contributing you agree that your contributions are licensed on the same terms as the repository: CC BY 4.0 for documents and MIT for code. Do not contribute material you do not have the right to license, including text from a firm's proprietary template library or from a paid drafting service.

## Process

Open an issue before a large change so the approach can be discussed. Small corrections can go straight to a pull request. For substantive legal changes, state in the pull request what the change is, what authority supports it, and what would have to be true for it to be wrong.
